Restaurant & Football Bar

The
Origin

Origin is a football bar and restaurant based in Seraulim, Goa. The restaurant is known for hosting regular events, football and cricket screenings, and attractive offers for customers. We started working with Origin from scratch, right from the time the restaurant started. Our association with the brand has continued for around 4+ years, during which we helped build and manage its social media presence through regular content, event promotions, and paid campaign support.

Client The Origin
Industry Restaurant & Football Bar
Service Social Media Management
Duration 4+ Years
The Challenge

IDENTIFYING THE DIGITAL GAPS

As Origin was a new restaurant, the main challenge was to build its social media presence from the ground up and create awareness for the brand, its offers, and its events.

The restaurant regularly hosted screenings, offers, and events, but these needed to be promoted consistently through stories, reels, and event creatives. There was also a critical need to maintain regular engagement on the page, increase followers, and attract more people for specific events in a competitive hospitality space.

The Objectives

Defining the GROWTH GOALS

The primary objective was to create awareness for Origin and promote its regular offers, events, and live screenings more effectively.

The client wanted to showcase its offers through regular story postings, create promo reels for selected events, and build visibility around football and cricket screenings. Another key goal was to increase page engagement, grow the follower base, and bring in more crowd for specific events through paid boosting.

The Strategy

BUILDING A STRONGER DIGITAL PRESENCE

We developed a social media strategy focused on the regular and timely promotion of Origin’s offers, events, and screenings to establish them as a go-to entertainment destination.

Regular story postings were created to keep the audience updated about offers and ongoing activities at the restaurant. Promo reels were produced for selected events where the client wanted to attract a larger crowd and create stronger awareness.

We also covered events such as football and cricket screenings to accurately showcase the live experience at the restaurant. Since we started with the brand from scratch, we carried out comprehensive shoots, including drone footage, to help build strong visual content for the page.

To increase visibility for key events, selected reels and story posters were boosted through targeted paid promotions. This helped create awareness for specific events and reach a larger audience beyond existing followers.
