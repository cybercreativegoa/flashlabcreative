Flashlab Begins

Ankith and Anuraj turned years of freelance experience into a registered studio in Margao. Flashlab Creative opened its doors two founders, one shared laptop, and a plan to bring proper creative work to Goa.

Flashlab Creative founded in Margao, Goa.

Growing the Team & Client Base

What started as a two person studio grew into a small creative team, taking on hospitality, F&B, and retail clients across Goa and building the roster that would define Flashlab's early reputation.
